# AEBC Shabibeh

The website for the AEBC church youth group ("shabibeh" means "youth" in Arabic).

It brings together the things a youth group needs during and between its meetings: a place to ask questions, an archive of past sermons, and a way to ask questions live while a talk is happening. The home page has four sections, and church leaders can switch any of them on or off when they're not in use.

## What you can do with it

- Ask a question, either anonymously or with your name. You can come back later to edit or delete your own question without making an account.
- Listen to recordings of past sermons.
- View and download the slides from past sermons.
- Send in questions during a live session and, when voting is turned on, vote for the questions you most want answered.

Leaders have a password-protected admin area where they can see and answer questions, run live sessions, turn sections on or off, keep a list of ideas for the site, and see simple visitor statistics.
